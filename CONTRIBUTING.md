# Contributing

Short version: bring scar tissue.

**Issues** — welcome for:
- a template line that misfired (what the agent did, what you expected)
- a rule that saved you, and the failure behind it
- typos and broken links

**Pull requests** — keep them small:
- One change per PR. Say what went wrong that the change prevents.
- Keep templates short, bracketed and tool-agnostic. A short honest file beats a long aspirational one.
- Thresholds, not adjectives. "Twice or more" beats "when there's duplication."
- No vendor lock-in in the core files. Tool-specific variants can go in their own folder.

The Chapter 2 excerpt in `docs/` is book text and isn't open for edits — typo reports are still welcome.

**Got a story that beats 67/68?** Open an issue with the "Story" template. I've been collecting them.

By contributing, you agree your contribution is released under the repo's [MIT licence](LICENSE).
